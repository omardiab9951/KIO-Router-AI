

Support: the customer reports an existing service problem, makes a complaint, or needs billing, account, or help with an existing order or service.

Sales: the customer mainly wants a new purchase, package, upgrade, price, quote, or promotion.

Mixed intent: if the message contains an existing problem AND interest in buying or upgrading, choose Support.

Negation: judge the customer's real need, not keywords. A customer who says they do not want to buy but needs something fixed is Support.
